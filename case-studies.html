Digital agency · Dubai, Business Bay · SEO and authority program

Search growth for Mighty Warners Technology

Mighty Warners is a 75-person digital agency in Business Bay, founded in 2014, offering SEO, SEM, social, web and app development to UAE brands. It sits in one of the most crowded search categories in the country: agencies competing for agency-related keywords across the Emirates.

The problem

Strong delivery, weak discoverability. The site ranked for its own brand name and little else, so new business depended on referrals and outbound.

What we did

  • Technical clean-up: crawl errors, page speed, duplicate service pages consolidated
  • Rebuilt the core service pages to answer buyer questions directly, with FAQ and service schema
  • A content program targeting agency-selection searches in Dubai, Sharjah and Abu Dhabi
  • Authority work: directory profiles, industry listings and guest placements that repeat one consistent entity description
  • Monthly rank and traffic tracking shared with the client

Outcome

Organic traffic grew 135% over the program and 46 target keywords moved into the top three positions. Inbound enquiries from search became a steady channel alongside referrals.

Apparel sourcing and product development · India, Dubai, London, Los Angeles

International search presence for Konception Designs

Konception Designs is an apparel sourcing and product development company with three factories, 980 machines and offices in Gurugram, Mumbai, Dubai, London and Los Angeles. It supplies finished garments and home goods to global retailers and high-end designers.

The problem

Buyers at international brands search for manufacturing partners by capability and country. Konception was invisible for those searches outside India, so sourcing enquiries came almost entirely through trade shows and personal networks.

What we did

  • Rebuilt the site structure around capabilities: product development, vendor selection, quality assurance, shipping
  • Country-specific landing pages for the UAE, UK and US markets with local contact details and schema
  • Content answering the questions sourcing managers actually ask: minimums, lead times, compliance, categories
  • Listings on international sourcing directories and trade platforms, with a consistent entity description
  • Tracking of enquiry source so the client could see which market each lead came from

Outcome

Inbound sourcing enquiries rose 120% and the site now ranks for manufacturing-partner searches in three countries, giving the sales team a pipeline that no longer depends on the trade-show calendar.
