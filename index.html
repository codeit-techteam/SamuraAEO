01

Audit the prompts

We test 50 real questions your buyers ask AI tools and record who gets named. You see the gap before you spend a dirham.

02

Fix the entity

AI engines reason about companies, not keywords. We make your name, description, address and services identical everywhere a model looks.

Rebuild the answer pages

Each key page answers one question in its first 50 words, with schema, FAQ blocks and llms.txt so a model can lift it as a citation.

Track and tighten

Weekly runs of the prompt set across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews. We find which sources each engine trusts and strengthen those.

Straight answers about Samura.

What is the difference between AEO, GEO and SEO?

SEO gets your pages ranked in Google and Bing results. AEO (answer engine optimisation) and GEO (generative engine optimisation) get your business named inside the answer an AI tool writes. The two overlap, because AI engines read the same web, but AEO adds entity work, structured data and off-site citations that classic SEO ignores. Samura does all three, with AI answers as the target.

Which AI platforms does Samura optimise for?

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews. Each engine trusts a slightly different set of sources, so we track all five separately every week and tune the work to whichever is falling behind.

How long before we appear in AI answers?

For specific, low-competition prompts, three to four weeks. For the main prompts in a competitive UAE category, two to three months of consistent work. Anyone promising five days has not done it.

Does Samura guarantee a number one position?

No, because AI answers change with phrasing, location and the day. Samura guarantees the work, the weekly tracking log, and that you will see your name appearing in answers that did not include you before. We show you the same log we keep for Samura.

Can Samura show real clients being cited by AI engines, before and after?

Yes. We keep a dated prompt log for every client with screenshots of the answers at the start and at each weekly check. You see the exact prompts, the exact engines and the exact wording. We also show you Samura's own log, since this company was our first case.

Does Samura do the technical work, or only reports?

We do the work. Schema, llms.txt, page rebuilds, Google Business Profile, directory listings, content and outreach are all delivered by our team. Reports exist to show you what changed, not as the product.

Does Samura support Arabic and English?

Yes. The Establish and Dominate programs include an Arabic FAQ layer and bilingual entity descriptions, because many UAE buyers prompt AI tools in Arabic and the engines reward sites that answer in both languages.

How does Samura measure results?

Four numbers, every week: how many of your tracked prompts name you, how many cite your site as a source, referral visits from AI tools in your analytics, and enquiries that mention finding you through an AI assistant. We set these up for you in the first week.

What is included in the price?

Exactly what the package lists, nothing hidden. Audit, entity setup, page rebuilds, articles, listings and tracking as stated for each program. If your site needs engineering work beyond the package, we tell you before starting and quote it separately.

Do we need SEO if we are doing AEO?

Yes, and it is part of every Samura program. AI engines pull from Google and Bing indexes, so a site that cannot rank in search is unlikely to be cited in answers. We do the SEO foundation as part of the AEO work rather than selling it twice.

Is there a long contract?

No. Programs run for one, two or three months as chosen. Longer programs cost less per month because the results compound, but nothing renews automatically.

Does Samura work with businesses outside Dubai?

Dubai is our focus and our first market. We take Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and other UAE clients where the category fits, and we handle India through our group company.

Who actually does the work?

Strategy and review sit with the two directors, IIT and IIM professionals who have advised groups like GMR and public-sector clients in India. Delivery is done by a bench of 50+ engineers and search specialists who have run ranking programs since 2018. You are never handed to a junior account manager.
